Growing up being the four eyed kid who wore glasses and wasn't tough,
I learned quickly that one's ability to build rapport and easily make 
friends went a long way against the mean kids, and as it turned out, 
I realized a healthy sense of humor was the key ingridient in making
those connections and disarming tense or awkward situations.

When time came for high school, I decided to attend the oldest and 
most prestigous technical high school in the country that was located 
right in the middle of a bustling capital's business center and sharing
a wall with the ministry of education itself.
